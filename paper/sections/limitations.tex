\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Internal validity.}
The ARML snapshot has one packet, one run per cell, incomplete heterogeneous
coverage, different model propensities to stop, and an LLM-judged CS. Parser
changes can alter short-answer scores without changing reasoning. Model,
protocol, call count, and early submission are not yet fully separated.

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
Turns approximate elapsed time but are not human minutes. API calls and token
windows differ from speech and paper. Lexical proxies can mismeasure semantic
communication, and rubric judges may share biases with evaluated models.
High message volume or balanced participation is not necessarily cohesion.

\paragraph{External validity.}
Public packets may be contaminated in model training. One mathematics packet
cannot support claims about programming, labs, writing, oral defense, or
multi-day research. LiveOIBench's oracle best-of-8 and official tests provide a
useful programming anchor, but independent candidates are not a collaborating
contest team \cite{zou2025liveoibench}.

\paragraph{Human--agent mismatch.}
Humans share physical devices, tire over hours, communicate by speech and
gesture, develop persistent skills and social trust, and may be assigned or
choose roles. Agents instead pay token/context costs, can be cloned, and do
not experience physical fatigue or laboratory manipulation. Internet,
submission feedback, penalties, and machine access can be modeled with
varying fidelity; speech bandwidth, five-hour fatigue, and wet labs currently
cannot. These differences require dimension-by-dimension fidelity labels, not
a blanket claim that an agent run reproduces a human contest.

\paragraph{Safety and reproducibility.}
Official tests and custom judges are trusted evaluator assets, not agent
context. Upstream host execution is unsafe for untrusted code. Our adapter is
non-executing; sandbox judging is a separate boundary. Missing official tests,
timestamps, multimodal assets, or isolation must be reported rather than
silently replaced by sample tests or host execution.
