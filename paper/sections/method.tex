\section{Methods and Baselines}
All comparisons should match model family, task, context access, and total
call/token/turn envelope unless the varied resource is the treatment. A
resource-matched single agent receives the team's per-turn call allowance;
handicap sweeps vary solo turns and calls to expose crossover points instead
of relying on one arbitrary budget.

Prompt-based memory, debate, self-consistency, subagent, and core team
protocols are implemented. The full crossed paper matrix has not been run.
The coach is restricted to preparation and the opening exchange. Subagent
workers are stateless and mutually invisible; their orchestrator aggregates
returns. Interactive team conditions expose shared history and workspace.
This contrast is necessary for interpreting cohesion rather than call count.
