\section{Conclusion}
Agent Olympiad reframes team-agent evaluation around contest-level constraints
and separates solving, strategy, communication, and cohesion. The repository
now supports the needed protocols and measurement interfaces, but the current
ARML evidence remains a preliminary, single-packet, early-stopping-confounded
snapshot. The next empirical milestone is a repeated, equal-budget matrix with
official or clearly scoped graders, followed by the solo--subagent--team
decomposition and human-fidelity analysis described here.
