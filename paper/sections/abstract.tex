\begin{abstract}
Agent Olympiad studies AI teams under the time, tool, information, submission,
and resource constraints of team competitions. The target is not unconstrained
single-problem solver ability. We organize evaluation around problem solving
and decomposition, strategy and resource allocation, communication, and
cohesion beyond independent subagents. The repository currently contains
contest rule cards, deterministic and rubric-based graders, collaboration
protocols, transcript metrics, and a preliminary single-packet ARML Local
matrix. Those numbers are reported only as confounded pilot evidence: many
solo runs stopped after one turn, and no broad paper-scale experiment is yet
complete. The proposed design separates task performance from deterministic
process proxies, a MultiAgentBench-style coordination score, and controlled
solo--subagent--team decompositions.
\end{abstract}
