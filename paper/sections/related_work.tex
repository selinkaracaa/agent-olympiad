\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Multi-agent evaluation.}
MultiAgentBench evaluates communication and planning in collaborative and
competitive agent settings and motivates reporting a coordination score
separately from task success \cite{zhu2025multiagentbench}. We adapt its
communication/planning rubric to olympiad chat and action logs, while also
adding deterministic transcript proxies. The adaptation is not a claim of
metric equivalence across domains.

\paragraph{Competitive-programming evaluation.}
LiveOIBench contains 403 recent informatics-olympiad problems from 72
competitions across 14 olympiads, official tests, and human standings
\cite{zou2025liveoibench}. Its generation/evaluation path uses multiple
independent candidates and official judging; we label hidden-score selection
explicitly as oracle best-of-8 rather than as autonomous team behavior.
