# ICPC-standard gap audit for the other competition rule cards

Date: 2026-08-17  
Scope: the 36 production bundles in data/rules other than icpc  
Mutation scope: research note only; no production rule JSON or code was changed by this audit

## Executive conclusion

The other bundles have the same three-file storage shape as ICPC, but they do not yet meet the ICPC card's evidence, collaboration, and evaluation standard. The right rewrite is not to copy ICPC's one-workstation programming rules. It is to reproduce ICPC's separation of:

1. source-grounded official constraints;
2. explicit simulation choices and unavailable mechanisms;
3. agent-facing coordination rules derived for that competition; and
4. official performance, rule compliance, collaboration quality, and repository availability as separate evaluation layers.

The current catalog-wide gaps are substantial:

- 35 of 36 non-ICPC cards lack agent_constraints; only wsc_writing has the field.
- 31 of 36 lack evaluation_guidance.
- 7 lack both rule_sections and information_policy; 12 lack deliberation; 17 lack communication.
- 25 lack an evaluator_id.
- All 36 lack the four explicit ICPC evaluation blocks: official_performance, rule_compliance, collaboration_quality, and current_repository_availability.
- Across 89 provenance source entries, none records authority, section/page pointers, or an edition/year field; only 38 have local_text. Fifty-three source entries have no descriptive title or merely repeat the URL.
- The same three simulation boilerplate sentences appear inside human_constraints in 35 cards. Those are agent/runtime rules, not official contestant rules, and belong in agent_constraints.
- A successful crawl is not proof of rule coverage. Current local captures include a CCDC redirect, a generic Hugging Face datasets page for DebateBench, a WMTC human-verification page, and internal fallback notes rather than first-party text for GCCH, Odyssey of the Mind, QANTA, and Science Olympiad.

The most consequential factual or scoping problems are not cosmetic: APPE permits any roster size with at most six active participants, not exactly five; NHSEB teams have three to seven members with at most five seated, not exactly five; the 2025 International History Bowl World Championship uses two- or three-player teams and no incorrect-answer deductions, not a four-player generic bowl model; HMMT November and February have different team and batch sizes; MIT Mystery Hunt has no official 8-12 person cap; and several numeric durations in the cards are estimates despite the source notes saying the time was not verified.

## Acceptance criteria for every rewritten bundle

A rewritten competition passes the ICPC content standard only if all criteria below hold.

### 1. Identity and edition are frozen

- competition_id identifies one ruleset, not a family of incompatible variants.
- The card records competition, track/category, level/division, host/season, rules edition, and effective date where any of them changes roster, timing, tools, format, or scoring.
- Mixed tracks require an explicit selector or separate rule IDs. Do not average or union incompatible variants such as HMMT November/February, Purple Comet middle/high school, WRO categories, or Science Olympiad events.

### 2. Every official claim is traceable to a primary source

- provenance.sources uses a descriptive title, official URL, authority=official (or authority=benchmark_owner for benchmark-native tracks), edition/date, retrieval date, local archive path, and section/page pointers.
- A landing page is not sufficient when it merely links a rulebook, host packet, technical appendix, rubric, or official Q&A. Archive and cite the binding attachment.
- A mirror, prior research report, dataset portal, or generated fallback note may locate a source but cannot by itself authorize a hard constraint.
- Unknown values remain null or explicitly unknown. No roster, duration, tool permission, scoring weight, or submission limit may be inferred from common practice.

### 3. competition.json contains only the correct rule layer

- team distinguishes full roster, active/seated participants, alternates, opponents, judges, coaches, and external roles.
- execution records official phase clocks separately from max_turns. turn_budget_basis labels the turn budget as an adaptation and never turns an approximate duration into an official fact.
- resources and allowed_tools are consistent, edition-specific, and default-deny when an official policy is unavailable.
- human_constraints contains atomic, contestant-facing official obligations only. Benchmark coverage notes, crawler failures, runner limitations, and maintainer instructions move to provenance or comparability.
- provenance.adaptations, provenance.proxy_limitations, and comparability.dimensions name every material mismatch, including missing opponents, live clocks, physical state, hidden judge state, multi-stage packets, and long-term workflows.

### 4. collaboration.json is competition-specific

- agent_constraints exists and translates the official topology plus simulation boundary into enforceable agent behavior.
- agent_roles mirrors equal teammates, official asymmetric roles, or adversarial/private coalitions as appropriate; it does not manufacture ICPC-style coder/navigator roles.
- information_policy describes what is actually shared and what remains private. Private debate coalitions, tossup no-conferral phases, external juries, and unseated team members must not be collapsed into one shared team memory.
- rule_sections labels statements as Official or Simulation choice, as ICPC does.
- deliberation and communication either model an official mechanism or are explicitly recorded as an adaptation. Message budgets are never presented as official unless the primary source defines them.

### 5. evaluation.json separates four kinds of judgment

- official_performance contains the exact official score/rank/rubric mechanics and edition-specific tie breakers.
- rule_compliance lists observable violations separately from score. It must not award official performance achieved through illegal tools or communication.
- collaboration_quality contains only benchmark research metrics, explicitly marked non-official, with anti-metrics that prevent rewarding message count, fixed roles, or equal airtime by themselves.
- current_repository_availability states which official state transitions and metrics the runner can actually enforce today.
- evaluation_guidance tells the evaluator to report these layers separately.
- submission distinguishes the real deliverable, the runner's accepted artifact, and the adaptation between them. evaluator_status remains deferred when the needed opponent, judge, physical apparatus, market, network, or rubric is absent.

### 6. Deterministic gates pass

- No unsourced numeric value is introduced.
- No human_constraint contains the terms benchmark, repo, crawler, proxy, current runner, not retrieved, or verify unless those words occur in a binding official rule.
- Every source-backed value has a stable section/page locator.
- The assembled card remains schema-valid; rule-card lint, storage tests, competition tests, evaluator references, task type, team size, and benchmark selectors agree.
- A semantic review confirms that official rules, simulation choices, and unavailable mechanisms cannot be mistaken for one another.

## Per-competition source coverage and rewrite blockers

## Deterministic rewrite procedure

1. Build a source ledger before touching JSON. One row per proposed fact: competition ID, field path, value, official document, edition, section/page, and whether the fact is official, a benchmark adaptation, or unavailable.
2. Resolve track identity first. Split or parameterize mixed variants before generating fields. A source conflict is a blocker, not an invitation to average values.
3. Generate competition.json from official ledger rows only. Move the three repeated simulation boilerplate lines and all benchmark/crawler prose out of human_constraints.
4. Generate collaboration.json from the verified interaction topology. Start with equal peers unless official roles differ; model opponents, judges, captains, active/seated participants, and private coalitions explicitly. Label every non-official coordination mechanism as Simulation choice.
5. Generate evaluation.json in four blocks: official performance, rule compliance, non-official collaboration research, and current repository availability. Leave evaluator_status deferred whenever the official judge state is absent.
6. Run a no-inference gate that rejects a numeric or enumerated claim without a source locator. It should also reject source objects without edition/authority/section metadata and human constraints containing maintainer language.
7. Validate schema, storage composition, rule-card lint, task-type/evaluator references, benchmark selectors, roster ranges, tool/resource consistency, and semantic separation. Then manually compare the assembled card with the source ledger.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Correct known factual/scoping errors first: ethics_bowl_appe, ethics_bowl_nhseb, history_olympiad, hmmt_guts, mystery_hunt, ioai_team, science_olympiad, wro, and the unsourced ARML/IEO/IJSO timing values.
