 PREVIOUS EOES 
 
 
 
 Until 2020, the European science Olympiads continued under the EUSO label.

Since 2021, the organisation of the contest is in the hands of a NPO (EOES association), whose members consist of the participating countries, represented by a Country Representative (CR) who functions at the same time as Country Coordinator (CC).

By clicking on the emblem of a previous olympiad, you will be directed to an overview page of the contest, where you can also download the assignments.
