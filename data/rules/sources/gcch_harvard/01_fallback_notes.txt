Global Case Competition at Harvard (fallback notes).

This is the university-level Global Case Competition at Harvard
(https://www.thecasecompetition.org/), NOT the high-school Harvard Crimson Global
Case Competition (https://casecomp.org/). An earlier crawl pointed at the retired
gcchatharvard.com domain and conflated the two events.

The organizer publishes no rulebook, terms document, or judging rubric, so only the
edition page is citable. Verified constraints live in
docs/rules_lowconf_2026-08-12.md and were merged into the rule card:

- Teams of 2 to 5 students from the same university; no individual entries.
- Round 1 deliverable is a written case deck built within up to three weeks of case
  release, submitted by the published deadline.
- Top 10 teams record a 5 to 10 minute video pitch; finalists face roughly 20
  minutes of live judge Q&A.

Unverified (no official statement exists): permitted research sources, software,
generative AI policy, plagiarism policy, outside assistance, and slide limits.
