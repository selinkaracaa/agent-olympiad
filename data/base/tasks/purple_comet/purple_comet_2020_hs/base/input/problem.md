Problem 25

A deck of eight cards has cards numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, in that order, and a deck of ﬁve cards has

cards numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, in that order. The two decks are riﬄe-shuﬄed together to form a deck with 13

might end up in the order 1122334455678 or 1234512345678 but not 1223144553678. Find the number of

possible sequences of the 13 numbers.
