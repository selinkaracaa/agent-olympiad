1. Introduction High above the ocean, the stone walls of an old fortress have protected history for hundreds of years. Beneath its towers, paths, and cobblestones lie stories of people, culture, animals, and artefacts that shaped life long ago. Over time, parts of this historic site have been damaged, buried, or forgotten.  Scientists, historians, and engineers are working together to protect this important heritage and they are using robots to help. Your team’s robot is sent into the fortress area to guide visitors, rebuild broken towers, clean important pathways and carefully transport valuable artefacts to the museum.  Along the way, your robot must work with precision and respect. Ancient objects must be handled gently, towers rebuilt correctly, and the site kept safe including the animals that live nearby. Every task helps to bring the past back to life and keeps history protected for the future.  Will your robot rise to the challenge and prove it deserves the title of Heritage Hero?
