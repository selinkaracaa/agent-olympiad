2025 CASE
FERRARI & PIRELLI
M&A GRAND PRIX, SPEED MEETS GRIP
As a buy-side analyst at IFSA Network, your team’s expertise and strategic acumen have once
again placed you at the center of a high-stakes opportunity. Last October, your Managing
Director was invited by Ferrari to the United States Grand Prix in Texas. As they witnessed
Ferrari’s dominance in the race, a unique deal opportunity emerged.
Ferrari, a legendary name in motorsport and luxury automotive engineering, is seeking to
strengthen its current position in the industriy by acquiring a strategic partner. During a private
discussion, Ferrari’s executive team highlighted Pirelli, the globally renowned tire manufacturer,
as a potential target.
Ferrari has granted IFSA Network exclusive rights to assess this potential acquisition, positioning
your team at the forefront of one of the most significant M&A opportunities in the motorsport and
luxury automotive sectors. This deal presents numerous strategic and financial challenges, with
Ferrari expecting a compelling recommendation. 
Your task is to develop a comprehensive pitch that addresses the following key questions:
Should Ferrari N.V. proceed with the acquisition of Pirelli & C SpA?
What competitive advantages would this deal provide Ferrari (e.g. Technological Innovation,
Branding, Market Positioning)?
Are there alternative targets that might better align with Ferrari’s long-term growth strategy?
Your Managing Director expects a to receive the deck by March 9th, 2025. Winning this internal
competition not only means securing a $10,000 bonus but also reinforcing IFSA’s position in the
market with this high profile transaction.

RECOMMENDED GUIDELINE
Outline your solution, and your most important arguments, in no more than one slide.
Introduce the reader to the problem by:
What would an acquisition mean in financial terms?
Is this deal a good idea from a strategic point of view?
Providing a brief history of the case
Analyzing both companies in terms of their history, operations, market position, and
strategic direction
Giving an overview of the relevant industry/sector

What do your results imply? Is this acquisition feasible?
What are the appropriate metrics that show if the deal is profitable or not?

Synergies, if the acquisition is successful?
Threats that may prevent a successful integration?
How would Ferrari benefit from Pirelli's capabilities?
Implications for the relevant industry/sector?
What potential risks could arise from this acquisition, and how might they impact the
overall implications of the deal?
