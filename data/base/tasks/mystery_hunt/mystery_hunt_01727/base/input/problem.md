Travels With Charlie -- COINHEIST Travels With Charlie Travels With Charlie -- Solved We've determined that KAOS agents have taken up positions in 25 cities worldwide, but a mind-eraser ray left our agents with no memories of their investigations. Fortunately, we have their Bleater logs, an internal communication mechanism used by CONTROL. (We tried to make the agents keep diaries, but Agent 86 kept losing his and letting them fall into KAOS' hands.) Unfortunately, after Bleater was hacked two months ago, we ordered agents to leave their bleats deliberately vague in case KAOS accessed them. It's up to you to figure out the cities our agents visited so that we can track down the KAOS bases. The agents traveling are: Agent 13, an agent who provides important support by hiding in mission-critical areas. Agent 38, aka Charlie Watkins, a master of disguise. Agent 86, Maxwell Smart. Agent 99 Some things that are useful to know: Agents are required to record their departure and arrival flight times. Agents are required to meet any time they are in the same city, and at least one agent in a meeting must record it in their bleats. Otherwise, agents can bleat whatever they want. Miraculously, all of the agents' flights were on-time. They took only commercially-operated flights. Finally, bleats record the local time at which the bleat was sent. This may be different from other similar communication systems you might be familiar with.
