Epcot Center: Bring Us Food, Yo Like all S.P.I.E.S. agents, you've trained at the C.I.A. &#8212; the Culinary Institute of America. You ran a training mission that required you to prepare a banquet for 300 of your enemies' closest friends. Now you'll prepare one for us. Please prepare a meal for headquarters using five foods that start with the same letter as your team's name. Your menu must have three courses (at least one vegetarian, at least one not) and serve six people. Please also include a written description of your dishes. If your team can't complete this assignment under its real name and needs an alias, please call us.
