You receive a treasure chest shut tightly using a chain with four locks. You pick one of the locks using a hairpin and a paperclip, and you manage to get the chain off the chest. You slowly open the lid -- inside are all the other puzzles in this round! Your team grabs all of the puzzles in the chest and happily runs off to solve them, leaving you with the locks, the chain, and the treasure chest itself. You look back at the lock you just picked, and you see a scrap of paper attached to the part of the shackle which had been hidden.
