# Power Round — Fall 2025

The Thue-Morse Sequence Fall 2025 ARML Power Contest The Background Mathematics abounds in curious sequences, and the Thue-Morse sequence is definitely among them. These questions will derive the sequence in a number of ways and demonstrate a few of its nifty properties and applications. In this set of questions, a word is any ordered list of symbols taken from the set {0, 1}. Note that order of symbols matters! The “empty word” consisting of no symbols is allowed, as are infinitely long strings of symbols. Thus, 010100, 111, 0, and 101010. . . are all words. So is the empty word consisting of no symbols. The words 10 and 01 are different because the symbols are in different orders. If W is a word of finite length, and X is any word, then W Xis simply the word consisting of the symbols in W followed by those in X. The word X is a subword of W if W = Y XZwhere Y and Z are any words (including the empty word). Thus 01 and 10 are both subwords of 101, but 11 is not a subword. The length of a word is the total number of symbols in it, including repeats. Thus the length of 10110 is 5. The Problems There are 40 points available on this contest.
