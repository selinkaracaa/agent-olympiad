# Power Round — Fall 2024

Find My Polynomial Fall 2024 ARML Power Contest The Background Alice and Bob are playing a game. Alice secretly chooses a polynomial p(x), perhaps with some restrictions such as requiring all coeﬃcients to be positive or requiring all roots to lie in a certain interval. Bob must try to ﬁgure out what polynomial Alice has chosen. Bob is allowed to ask Alice to evaluate her polynomial for any real input x, and Alice must answer with the correct value of p(x). Such an exchange will be called a question. Depending on the restrictions on Alice’s polynomial, Bob might have to ask many questions before he can ﬁnd the polynomial, or he might be able to do so with just one question. Your challenge is to ﬁnd out how many questions will be needed in each case. For the purposes of this problem, a polynomial is an expression of the form p(x) = akxk + ak−1xk−1 +··· + a1x + a0 where all of the coeﬃcients ai are real numbers and all exponents are nonnegative integers. This polynomial has degree k if and only if ak ̸= 0, otherwise the text will specify that the degree is at most k. Should it be necessary, the polynomial all of whose coeﬃcients are zero will be said to have undeﬁned degree. A monomial is a polynomial with just one non-zero term, akxk, with ak̸= 0. Alice and Bob both have access to a very fast computer that can solve any equation or system of equations to any desired degree of accuracy instantaneously. You may use the fact that a polynomial of degree at most k that has k + 1 or more distinct roots must be the zero polynomial, p(x) = 0. The Problems There are 40 points available on this contest. The numbers at the end of each problem are the number of points that problem is worth. These are also indicated on the cover sheet, which should be the front page of your solution packet.
