# History

Led by Jacques Laffitte and Francois Guizot, it arose largely from the appointment of Jules de Polignac as premier. Its support from Adolphe Thiers resulted in the reduction of the electorate, rigid press control, and dissolution of the chamber, the so-called "Ordinances" directly preceding it. But after a massive citizens' protest, Henri, the duke of Bordeaux, took the place of the abdicating Charles X, although Henri too would be replaced by the duke of Orléans, later known as Louis Philippe. For ten points, name this 1830 coup d'état, named for the month in which it took place.
