# Literature

This story written in the form of letters to a "Mr. A. Ward" takes place in a town called Boomerang. Among the creatures in this story are a "yaller one-eyed cow;" the "fifteen-minute nag;" and a "small bull-pup" whose signature move was hanging on other dogs' hind legs until they gave up, Andrew Jackson. Simon Wheeler says he believes that all a certain animal wanted was education and he could do anything, a belief first espoused by Jim Smiley. For ten points, name this Mark Twain story about Dan'l Webster, an amphibian with excellent leaping ability.
