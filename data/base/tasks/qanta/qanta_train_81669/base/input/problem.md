# Mythology

She traded Ganymede to Zeus in exchange for immortality for her lover, Tithonus, but without eternal youth, however, Tithonis eventually became a cicada. The daughter of Hyperion and Theia, this Greek goddess gave birth to the stars and winds. Name, for 10 points, this rosy-fingered goddess, the sister of Selene and Helios, who the Romans called Aurora.
