# Fine Arts

The title character of this opera is the son of Gamuret and Herzeleide and is also known as Falparsi or the Innocent Fool. Ordered by the composer never to be performed anywhere but the Bayreuth theater, this opera tells of the struggle for the Holy Grail between Klingsor and Amfortas. For ten points name this Richard Wagner opera, whose title character is the father of Lohengrin.
