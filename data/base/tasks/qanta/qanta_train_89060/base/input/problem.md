# Science

Maxwell's Relations for this quantity imply that its partial derivative with respect to pressure at constant entropy is equal to the system volume. This property is conserved in a throttling process, and is used for the thermodynamic analysis of an open chemical system because it implicitly includes a flow work term. For 10 points, name the thermodynamic property that is a state function according to Hess's Law, is equal to the internal energy plus the product of the volume and pressure, and is represented by a capital H.
