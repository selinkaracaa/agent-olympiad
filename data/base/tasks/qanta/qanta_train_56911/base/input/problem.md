# Mythology

He was primarily a pastoral god with the epithet "Shepherd." One myth says that he was the son of Duttur and Enki while another says his mother was Ninsun, the Wild Lady Cow. His marriage to the goddess Inanna and his death at the hands of demons in the underworld formed two major festivals. His death and rising were celebrated in Babylon at festivals commemorating the yearly cycle of vegetation.
