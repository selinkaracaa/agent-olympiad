# Mythology

In the Gylfaginning, High says that Gefion is attended only by the spirits of people with this status. Examples of these people in the Kalevala include the primeval deity Luonnotar and the woman Marjatta. A Jotunn named Modgud who guards the bridge over the river Gjoll, the Vanir goddess Gullveig, and Frigg's attendant Fulla all have this quality. Sigurd slept with his sword between himself and Brynhild in order to demonstrate that Brynhild remained one of these figures. For 10 points, give this status emphatically not shared by Freyja, who slept with many partners.
