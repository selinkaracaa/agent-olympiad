# Religion

They became a worldwide movement under Nathan Knorr in the 1940s and they are controlled by a twelve member governing body based in Brooklyn. Non- trinitarians, they believe in conditional immortality and an earthly paradise. Their second leader, Joseph Rutherford, adjusted the teachings laid out in publications like Millenial Dawn and the Watchtower magazine. FTP identify this religious group also known as the Bible Students who originated in 1870s Pennsylvania under Charles Taze Russell.
