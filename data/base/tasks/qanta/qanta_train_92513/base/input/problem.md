# History

It was the title of a humour magazine published in 1971 and 1972 by Jeffrey Darcey,  featuring Stanley Berneche's Captain Canada and sidekick Beaverboy. The phrase  originated on February 16, 1971, after John Lundrigan of Gander-Twillingate asked about  unemployment relief and was interrupted with an unspoken but mouthed response. Asked  by reporters outside the House to respond to Opposition allegations of a "disgraceful" and  "unacceptable" "obscenity", for 10 points, what did Prime Minister Pierre Trudeau claim to have been thinking when he moved his lips?
