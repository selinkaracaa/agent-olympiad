EOES 2025

EOES 2025 was hosted by Zagreb University , where the competitors had access to the laboratories of the Faculty of Science for their assignments.

As previous years it was a joyful reunion for many of the mentors and an unforgettable event for the youngsters from 22 European Union countries and one guest team from Ukraine.

Once again the organisers managed to pair some nice experiments with two original themes: Waters of Croatia: From the Emerald Rivers to the Deep Blue Sea and Chemistry, Physics Biology - Tied by Time and Change.

TASK 1

Subtitle
In this task, the participants faced questions about freshwater microfauna and had to determine water purity based on the presence of certain invertebrates.

They also had to determine the salt concentration of the water in salt pans and find out which salt plants enriched the extracted salt with iodine.

TASK 2

Subtitle
Chemical reactions, whether they take place in a split second or over millions of years, reshape matter.

Biological systems evolve, grow and adapt through time-bound processes.

Physical forces drive changes from the smallest atomic movements to the vast motions of galaxies.

In all sciences, time and change are threads that connect discoveries, explain patterns and reveal the dynamic nature of life and the universe.

In this second assignment, this was beautifully demonstrated through a number of biological, chemical and physical experiments.
