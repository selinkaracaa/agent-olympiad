## Contest briefing

Odyssey of the Mind. The task represents a judged creative performance through a written proxy. Mixed or unresolved rulesets: the runner permits 5 to 7 active agents and defaults to 7. Collaborate only within the team under contest rules. Banned during the contest: internet access, calculators and running code. Paper and pencil are always available. Work only from the materials provided with the problem. Submit one shared long term performance; Submit the complete team deliverable requested by the problem statement. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.

## Binding contestant constraints

- Creative long-term / spontaneous problem solving under OM constraints.
- No outside assistance producing the solution for the team.
- Scoring packets may be incomplete in public data; stay faithful to the public synopsis.
- Teams of up to 7 members under an adult coach; recommend ≥5; all who contributed stay on roster.
- Long-Term problems solved over weeks/months; Spontaneous solved on-site; Style scoring component.
- Outside assistance restrictions are central (coaches may not contribute ideas to the solution — details in Program Guide).
- Full scoring packets / long-term specifics often membership-gated; public synopses only for many past problems.
