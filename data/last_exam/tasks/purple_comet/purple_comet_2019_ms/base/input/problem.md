Problem 9

A semicircle has diameter AD with AD = 30. Points B and C lie on AD, and points E and F lie on the

arc of the semicircle. The two right triangles △BCF and △CDE are congruent. The area of △BCF is
m√n, where m and n are positive integers, and n is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find m + n.


Problem 10

Let N be the greatest positive integer that can be expressed using all seven Roman numerals I, V, X, L, C,

D, and M exactly once each, and let n be the least positive integer that can be expressed using these

numerals exactly once each. Find N −n. Note that the arrangement CM is never used in a number along

with the numeral D.

Problem 20

Harold has 3 red checkers and 3 black checkers. Find the number of distinct ways that Harold can place

these checkers in stacks. Two ways of stacking checkers are the same if each stack of the ﬁrst way matches

a corresponding stack in the second way in both size and color arrangement. So, for example, the 3 stack

arrangement RBR, BR, B is distinct from RBR, RB, B, but the 4 stack arrangement RB, BR, B, R is the

same as B, BR, R, RB.
