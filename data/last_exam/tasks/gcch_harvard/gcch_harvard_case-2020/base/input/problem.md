AMAZON & NETFLIX
WINNING THE STREAMING WARS
You are a team of successful bankers working at IFSA Network. Today you have been
informed by your Managing Director that Amazon would like to expand its content
library in order to win the 'streaming wars'.
 
Due to its past success, the IFSA Network managed to be the only one on the deal.
The problem is that you are not the only team working on the project. 
 
Your challenge will be to find an answer to this question: 
 
Should Amazon acquire Netflix? 
 
The question is broad, but the possibilities boundless. You will need to find the most
appropriate answer. You must provide useful insights and you must rigorously
support them, and ultimately you must convince the board of directors.
Should Amazon acquire Netflix?  Should it acquire it partially? Or, should it find
another target?
 
Beat the internal competition and get your $10,000 bonus!
 
 
 
 
 

RECOMMENDED GUIDELINE
Outline your solution, and your most important arguments, in no more than
one slide.
Introduce the reader to the problem by…
What would an acquisition mean in financial terms?
Is this deal a good idea from a strategic point of view?
providing a brief history of the case
analyzing both companies in terms of their history, operations, market position,
and strategic direction
giving an overview of the relevant industry/sector 
Value 100% of Netflix's equity using the DCF and/or the multiples approach and
discuss the implications of your result. Is an acquisition feasible for Amazon? What
ROI could they expect?
Synergies, if Netflix can be successfully integrated
Threats that may prevent a successful integration
How would Netflix fit in Amazons' portfolio
Implications for the relevant industry/sector
Topics of this chapter may include, but are not limited to:
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION
CHAPTER 2: FINANCIAL DUE DILIGENCE  
CHAPTER 3: NON-FINANCIAL DUE DILIGENCE    

CHAPTER 4: CONCLUSION
In this final chapter, you should derive your solution by balancing the pro and
counter arguments for the deal. If you come to the conclusion that Amazon
should proceed, provide a brief outline of a possible deal and the next steps to
follow. If you believe that Amazon should not go for the deal, provide some
alternative investment strategies it could undertake to acquire similar
capabilities, and renew its brand.
This only serves as a recommendation of procedure. You are not constrained by it in
any way, and are encouraged to explore further avenues and thought processes.
