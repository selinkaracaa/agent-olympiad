We’ve got a capacitor formed by two separated conducting plates. We charge it and disconnect
it from the voltage source. How many times larger will the charge we can get from the capacitor
be, if we break it into four identical pieces and place them on top of each other without rotating
them in any way?

Problem X.3 . . . yoghurt
Jáchym eats half a kilogram of yoghurt for dinner every day. The yoghurt contains a mass
fraction w = 10−4 of a certain radioactive isotope with half-life 26 days. How many grams of
this isotope will be present in Jáchym’s body every day before dinner, if he’s been eating this
way for a long time? Assume that the isotope doesn’t leave his body in any way other than
through radioactive decay.
