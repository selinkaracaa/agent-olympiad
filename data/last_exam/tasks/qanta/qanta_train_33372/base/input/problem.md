# Mythology

He fathered Vidar with the giantess Grid, and has only one eye because he traded the other for a drink from the Well of Wisdom. He has two wolves named Geri and Freki and two (*) ravens named Huginn and Muninn, and he can see the entire world from Hlidskalf (LID-skalf), his throne. He will be killed by Fenrir at Ragnarok, and owns the horse Sleipnir, the spear Gungnir, and the ring Draupnir. For 10 points, name this chief god of the Norse pantheon.
