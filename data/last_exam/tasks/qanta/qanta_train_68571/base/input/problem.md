# Literature

The ending of this novel consists of the story being told through the protagonist's diary entries. Ending with the protagonist enrolling in University College, it also shows him listening to arguments about Parnell at a family Christmas dinner, hearing the profound lectures of Father Arnall, and having a sexual experience with a Dublin prostitute. Beginning with "a moocow coming down along the road", it was originally titled Stephen Hero. For 10 points, name this James Joyce novel that shows the early years of Stephen Daedalus, whose adventures were continued in Ulysses.
