# Religion

This man accidentally destroys several of his father's statues and blamed the destruction on an idol. In one story, he meets and is blessed by the priest-king Melchizedek. This man twice disguises his wife as his sister to escape dangerous situations. He fails to find ten good people in Sodom and Gomorrah in order to save his nephew Lot. He was promised descendants greater in number than the stars, this figure's family included his wife Sarah and his son Isaac, whom he almost sacrifices. For 10 points, name this Jewish Patriarch.
