# Science

When the kinetic energy equals the potential energy in a system, the Lagrangian takes this value. The magnetic force on an electron moving parallel to a magnetic field is equal to this value. This is the amount of spin on the Higgs boson, as well as the amount of (*) mass in a photon and the amount of pressure in a perfect vacuum.
