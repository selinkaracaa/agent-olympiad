# Geography

One mayor of this city accidentally set his hair on fire with a blowtorch during a photo op, and that mayor, Ralph Perk, was succeeded by a Democrat who is now mostly known for his British redhead wife. This city staged the Great Lakes Exposition in the late 1930s to promote tourism, and race riots in this city began when Joyce Arnett was shot by police. The Hough ["huff"] Riots took place in 1966 in this city, where Dennis Kucinich was mayor. It was the first major city to have an African-American mayor, and that mayor was Carl Stokes.  This city's economic resurgence under mayor George Voinovich was spearheaded by the construction of a building designed by I. M. Pei.
