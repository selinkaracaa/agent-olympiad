# Science

The seeds of these types of plants contain iron, B Vitamins, and consist of high percentages of protein. Classified as being members of the Fabaceae family, they are divided into forage and grain types. Because of their symbiotic (*) relationship with the rhizobia bacteria, these plants are often used to enrich soil without the use of fertilizer. Consisting of members that have soybeans, peanuts, and peas as seeds, FTP, identify these types of plants that are notable for their nitrogen fixation abilities.
