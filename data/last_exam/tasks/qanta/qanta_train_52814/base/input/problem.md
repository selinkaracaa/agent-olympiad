# History

Among her publications is "What Every Mother Should Know". A  practitioner of free lover, this New York teacher and nurse was  horrified by the squalor of living conditions brought about by  uncontrolled reproduction among the lower classes. In 1916 she was  arrested after opening the first birth-control clinic in the  country. FTP, name this founder of the American Birth Control  League, and the first honorary chairwoman of Planned Parenthood.
