# History

A Supreme Court case addressing this act declared that words in laws "must exist for somepractical purpose" and refused to overturn the conviction of John Bitty. Chicago district attorneyEdwin Sims was a campaigner for this law, which was the culmination of a hysteria begun inMcClure's magazine by George Kibbe Turner and blamed on "Russian Jews." Chuck Berry, CharlieChaplin, and Jack (*) Johnson were among the celebrities charged under this law. The namesake of thisact also sponsored a 1910 law allowing the Interstate Commerce Commission to regulatetelecommunications, with Stephen Elkins.
