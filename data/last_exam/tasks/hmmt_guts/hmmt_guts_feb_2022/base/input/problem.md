   the product is prime can be expressed as ab for relatively prime positive integers a and b, compute 100a+b.

   tenth. Real numbers a and b satisfy ⟨a⟩+ [b] = 98.6 and [a] + ⟨b⟩= 99.3. Compute the minimum possible
   value of [10(a + b)].

   (Here, any number equally between two integers or tenths of integers, respectively, is rounded up. For
   example, [−4.5] = −4 and ⟨4.35⟩= 4.4.)
