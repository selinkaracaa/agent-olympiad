21. [14] Bob knows that Alice has 2021 secret positive integers x1, . . . , x2021 that are pairwise relatively prime.
   Bob would like to ﬁgure out Alice’s integers. He is allowed to choose a set S ⊆{1, 2, . . . , 2021} and ask
    her for the product of xi over i ∈S. Alice must answer each of Bob’s queries truthfully, and Bob may use
    Alice’s previous answers to decide his next query. Compute the minimum number of queries Bob needs
    to guarantee that he can ﬁgure out each of Alice’s integers.
