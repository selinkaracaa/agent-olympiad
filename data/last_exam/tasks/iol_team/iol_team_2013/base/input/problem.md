# Team Contest — Georgian

en Eleventh International Olympiad in Linguistics Manchester (Great Britain), 22–26 July 2013 Team Contest Problem Fifteen years ago the British poet, critic, and biographer Martin Seymour-Smith compiled a certain list. The following two pages contain that list in a Georgian translation, written in the ancient Nuskhuri alphabet (9th century). Translate as much of it as you can into English. —Ivan Derzhanski
