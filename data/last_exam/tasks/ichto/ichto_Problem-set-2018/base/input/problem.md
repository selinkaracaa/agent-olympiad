# Problem-set-2018

Problems set 
of the International Chemistry Tournament 2018
Problem 1. Redox memory
Propose a redox chemical memory device of at least 
one kilobyte of memory. A storage of information should 
be based on reduction-oxidation reactions where an 
oxidized form of the memory cell components equals 
“one” and the reduced form equals “zero”. Explain the 
mechanism of writing and reading for your module. How 
fast are these processes? What is the size of your 
device? How long could it store the information without a 
risk of losing it?
Problem 2. Fluorescent trafﬁc light
Fluorescent dyes emit light of a strictly deﬁned wavelength, 
which depends on the molecular and electronic structure of 
the dye molecules. Propose a multicomponent system, that 
will gradually change the color of its glow after excitation. 
The system should qualitatively change the character of the 
glow at least twice between three different colors.
Problem 3. Arodnap and ﬂying islands
On the planet Arodnap, the average atmospheric 
temperature is about 60 K and it doesn’t ﬂuctuate 
much during day and night. Unfortunately there is no 
life, but you may ﬁnd there some levitating islands, 
like the ones from the Avatar movie. Of course, the 
planet Arodnap is ﬁctional. However, try to come up 
with a plausible mechanism of the formation of such 
islands. Which processes could lead to the formation 
of speciﬁc minerals, that are in charge of these 
effects? Suggest, based on this mechanism, the 
features of Arodnap: its geological history, a 
composition of the atmosphere and crust and so on.

Problem 4. Periodic table of organic groups
If someone needs to prove the predictive 
power of science, Mendeleev’s periodic 
table is an obvious choice: it paved the 
way of chemistry from alchemy to hard 
science, and correctly predicted features 
of the Universe which had been 
completely unheard of (i. e. gallium and 
germanium).
It is not only that new periodic rules are 
established day by day. The periodic table 
of chemistry also inspired the standard 
model of physics, which is the “periodic 
table” of elementary particles (electrons, quarks and bosons). But can this idea be extended in 
other directions?
Construct a periodic table of organic groups. The table should feature the well-known functional 
groups, but you might consider including some exotic ones, too. The main criterion is that the 
position of each group should be unambiguous, and you must be able to show the rule of 
periodicity (how the members of the same column resemble one another). Any predictions you can 
make are highly appreciated. The format is up to you.

Replace the spins with nanosized magnets (for

Problem 6. Superbubble gum 
A villain from the “Despicable Me 3” movie uses some 
kind of superpowered gum to levitate things. For 
example this gum is capable of lifting cargo ship into 
the air by forming large lighter-than-air bubbles. 
Probably this happens after the gum is set in contact 
with water.  
Give a suggestion of the material capable of forming 
such bubbles after contact with some liquid. Evaluate 
maximum lifting capacity of your gum. It should at least 
be able to levitate itself.
Problem 7. Never-changing perfume
Perfumes are often based on combinations of essential oils. 
For example, neroli oil which produced from the blossom of 
the bitter orange tree (Citrus aurantium subsp. amara). Since 
XVII century it is widely applied in perfumery. Neroli is even 
thought to be part of Coca-Cola ﬂavour combination. People 
often mix this oil with citrus oil or similar substances. The 
combination has a truly nice aroma. Unfortunately, after some 
time the smell of the neroli and citrus oil loses its freshness, 
and the mix itself may even change in colour.
What is happening with the mixture? How one can avoid loss 
of the aroma freshness and stop unwanted processes?
Problem 8. Antisalt
When cooking people often face a problem with the 
dosage of salt. While low saltness is easy to correct, 
high saltness is problematic to make back normal. 
Suggest a chemical method which could remove 
excess salt from food or at least mask it. The food must 
remain edible.

Problem 9. Molecular security
Nowadays the issue of protecting intellectual property is completely 
actual. What if you want to protect individual molecules from a fake? 
Describe a method which allows you design the sufﬁciently secure 
cypher to mark the molecules of the substance produced by you. 
How can a consumer verify the molecule is not fake?
Problem 10. Safe construction toy
Suggest a material for construction toys “Lego” which will not 
harm when a child accidently swallow its details. The details 
of the constructor must preserve their properties (hardness, 
stability and other physical properties).
Problem 11. Molecular spring
Propose organic compound of non-biological 
origin, the molecules of which has a spiral 
shape. It should be able to compress or stretch 
when it is exposed to external chemical or 
physicochemical factors. Evaluate its stiffness 
measured in N/m.
Problem 12. Chemical scissors
You would like to set up an experiment where one 
substance reacts with another, called “chemical 
scissors”. As a result of an experiment, we obtain easily 
separable  two-component  mixture  of  two  
compounds.The initial substance should be colourless, 
and the formed compounds should have different 
colours . Propose the details of such experiment 
(compounds, conditions, concentrations etc).
