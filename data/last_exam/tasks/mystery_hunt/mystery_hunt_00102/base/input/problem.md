Mystery Hunt 2000, Puzzle 3.3 3.3 Mothers Know Best Back to hunt | Back to puzzle set Dorothy and the Tin Man passed by a "DEF TUV TUV OPER OPER" poster as they walked down the Infinite Corridor. "Tin Man, who do you talk to when that heart of yours needs some advice?" "Why, my mother, of course! Trouble is, she hasn't gotten her heart yet; she's been on the transplant list for years. But nonetheless, she somehow manages to have all the answers." The Tin Man paused for a moment, then added, "That is, unless I call her before 10 a.m. or after 10 p.m. (her time). In that case she won't even pick up the phone. Does your mother do that?" "Tin Man, you know I'm an orphan!" "Oh right. Sorry. Well, you could always call one of MIPB's mothers. I'm sure they wouldn't mind."
