Warrant 3.1 Warrant 3.1 We have reason to believe that the crook is particularly fond of gingerbread. Thus, to apprehend the crook, we are requesting a sculpture of an animal, made out of gingerbread, to set as bait. Sources have determined that the crook has a sweet tooth, and thus you are to put on your gingerbread a lot of icing and candied ornamentation. The sculpture must be three-dimensional (no cookie cutter cut-outs), and again, the sculpture must be in the shape of an animal. The minimum dimensions of the object should be 6" x 6" x 6"; please add an additional 1 cubic inch for every two team members you have after 8. The object may be hollow. You may deliver the bait to ACME headquarters at any time. Good luck. Solution
